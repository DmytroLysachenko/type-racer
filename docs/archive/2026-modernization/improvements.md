## Conditional product and infrastructure decisions

A verified public leaderboard, prizes or durable shared results require server-observed scoring, replay protection, a shared datastore, retention rules, distributed abuse controls and an identity policy. Cross-device history or accounts require a privacy and migration design. No datastore credentials, account provider or current product requirement for verified competition exists in this repository. A process-local limiter or client score check would not satisfy these requirements. The [verified-results and account-sync proposal](adr/0002-verified-results-proposal.md) defines the transport, datastore, ownership, migration and test gates to review before implementation. Keep current peer standings explicitly unverified.

A dedicated settings page, sound effects, achievements and daily challenges are not part of the current focused product. Theme and last-used practice mode are the settings backed by real behavior. Additional languages or exercises can extend `lib/content/practice-material.ts` with rights-reviewed text. The separate practice route and its content were approved by the owner on 2026-10-15; later user direction authorized the additional local work documented above.
